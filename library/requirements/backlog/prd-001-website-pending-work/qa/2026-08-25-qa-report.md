1. **`EMC_PROFILES` duplicated between `index.tsx` and `TeamMembers.tsx`.** I agree this is the correct call. 2. **`/team` ships with no `Person` or `ProfilePage` structured data.** I agree this is out of scope. No criterion in `prd-001a` mentions schema, and inventing one during a QA pass would be exactly the scope creep the Alignment axis exists to catch. It is a genuine AEO opportunity on a page of named, NMLS-licensed people, and `seo-aeo-guardian` should own it as follow-up work rather than as a defect against this run.
