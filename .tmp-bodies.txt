<h1>How to access your home equity without refinancing your first mortgage</h1>
<p class="lead">Short answer: you don't have to refinance. A <b>HELOC</b> or a <b>fixed second mortgage</b> lets you pull cash from your equity while leaving your first mortgage — and its low rate — completely untouched.</p>

<main><div class="wrap">
<div class="byline">By <b>Ahoo Khalessi</b>, Division Manager &amp; Loan Officer, NMLS #2239510 &nbsp;·&nbsp; June 2026</div>
<div class="post-body">
<p>If you locked a low rate on your first mortgage, a cash-out refinance would replace it at today's higher rate — an expensive way to get cash. The better move is usually a <b>second lien</b> that sits behind your first mortgage, so your first loan never changes.</p>
<h2>Two ways to do it</h2>
<p>A <b>HELOC</b> is a revolving line of credit you draw from as needed, usually at a variable rate — good for ongoing or flexible needs. A <b>fixed second mortgage</b> gives you a one-time lump sum at a fixed rate and payment — good for a known, one-time expense like debt consolidation or a renovation.</p>
<h2>You may not even need an appraisal</h2>
<p>At CTCequity, you can often access <b>up to $400,000 with no appraisal required</b> on a HELOC or fixed second — faster and lower cost. For larger needs, we place HELOCs and fixed seconds <b>up to $4 million</b>, which is rarely available elsewhere.</p>
<h2>How much can you borrow?</h2>
<p>Most programs allow a combined loan-to-value of roughly 80–90% across your first mortgage and the new line, depending on credit and the lender. With access to 160+ lenders, we match your scenario to the program that allows the most.</p>
<h2>The bottom line</h2>
<p>Keep your low first-mortgage rate. Use a HELOC or fixed second to get cash or clear debt without disturbing it. If you'd like your real numbers, <a href="ctc-equity-website.html#getstarted">tell us your scenario</a> — no full application to start.</p>

function bodyToHtml(raw){
  var lines=raw.split(/\n/), out=[], list=[];
  function flush(){ if(list.length){ out.push('<ul>'+list.map(function(li){return '<li>'+li+'</li>';}).join('')+'</ul>'); list=[]; } }
  var para=[];
  function flushP(){ if(para.length){ out.push('<p>'+para.join(' ')+'</p>'); para=[]; } }
  lines.forEach(function(ln){
    var t=ln.trim();
    if(t===''){ flushP(); flush(); return; }
    if(t.indexOf('## ')===0){ flushP(); flush(); out.push('<h2>'+inline(t.slice(3))+'</h2>'); return; }
    if(t.indexOf('- ')===0){ flushP(); list.push(inline(t.slice(2))); return; }
    para.push(inline(t));
  });
  flushP(); flush();
  return out.join('\n');
}
function inline(s){ // allow **bold**
  s=esc(s);
  return s.replace(/\*\*(.+?)\*\*/g,'<b>$1</b>');
}
